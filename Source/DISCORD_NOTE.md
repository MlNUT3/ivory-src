# grim — member guide

## Getting access
1. Ask the owner for an **access token** (`grim-xxxx-xxxx`). Tokens are one-use — don't share yours.
2. Open the panel → **Create account** → username (3+ characters) + password (6+ characters) + your access token → Create.
3. Your token is also your login for the client — keep it somewhere safe.

## Running the client
1. You get the client as a **password-protected archive** from the owner. Extract it wherever you like.
2. On the panel, click **Copy** next to your access token (Client tab).
3. Double-click the app — it takes the token straight from your clipboard, no typing. (If it didn't find one, a small box pops up — paste it there and press ok.) It saves once it connects, and never asks again.
4. Join a game — it attaches on its own. Everything (aimbot, triggerbot, ESP, players, configs) is controlled from the panel in your browser.

## Everyday use
- **Load / Unload** — top bar of the panel.
- **Configs** — Config tab: save your setup, "Set auto" makes it load every run.
- **Players** — World tab: live list, minimap, spectate.
- **Stop playing** — Client tab → **Close exe**.

## Panic key
Set it under Settings → Tools. Pressing it instantly kills the client and wipes every trace of it (registry included). The exe file itself stays on disk — run it again to come back.

## Before a PC check
1. Hit the panic key (or panel → Client → Self destruct).
2. **Reboot.**
That's it — nothing starts on boot, nothing persists anywhere on the disk, nothing is left running. Nothing to find.

## Notes
- **One device per token** — the first machine you run it on binds to your account. New PC? Ask the owner to reset your HWID (Users tab).
- **DMs** — Chat → Direct. Someone has to accept your request before they can see anything you send, same for you.
- Keep the archive password and the exe **off shared drives** — your machine only.
